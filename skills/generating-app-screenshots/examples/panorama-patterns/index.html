Panorama patterns

15 ways to connect a 5-slide set. Signature patterns first: light, type and lines carry the connection, so no phone is ever cut by the store gap. Each is one line of config: "panorama": { "pattern": "peek" }.

Store rules behind the risk levels
  • The App Store and Play show each screenshot as a separate rounded card with a gap. A phone across a seam gets split by that gap, so check the store view.
  • Most shoppers only see slides 1–3, often as small search thumbnails. Slide 1 must work alone, so every pattern keeps it self-contained.
  • Backgrounds (gradients, skies, ridges, waves) can flow freely. Captions must never cross a seam; the renderer warns when one does.
  • iPhone and iPad need separate compositions, because their aspect ratios differ.
